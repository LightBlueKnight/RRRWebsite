Who We Are

Red Rock Robotics is a FIRST Robotics Competition program based out of West High School in Salt Lake City, Utah. Our program houses two FRC teams, 3006 (Red Rock Robotics) and 2726 (Red Pebble Rebels). Both of our teams share resources, mentorship, and space. We want all students to have access to hands-on learning and believe that everyone has a place in STEM no matter the background. We teach students everything from design, machining, and software development to fundraising, data analysis, and marketing.

We have over 40 active members across our program and 5 amazing coaches and mentors. Through these dedicated students, mentors, and coaches Red Rock Robotics has grown into one of the most successful robotics programs in Utah.

Team 3006

3006 Red Rock Robotics is our main competition team. Students on this team work at a very high competition level, building, designing, wiring, and coding the robot with the aim of being the most competitive as possible. Students develop their skills on this team after having previous experience with FRC, whether on our team or another. The team has had major national and international success including being the second pick of the winning alliance in the Archimedes Division at the World Championship in 2026, winning the Utah Regional in 2026, and winning the Denver Regional in 2024.

Team 2726

2726 Red Pebble Rebels was founded in the 2026 FRC season with the goal of getting as many students as possible hands-on experience with the robot, no matter the skill level. As well as acting as a natural pipeline to 3006. For most students on 2726 this is their first time making and competing with a robot in FRC. Giving these students an amazing opportunity to experiment and learn. The team competes at our local regionals and has historically done well. In its first year the team captained an alliance at the Utah Regional.

Our Subteams

Hardware - Machines, CAD, designs and builds the robot

Electrical - Designs and wires the robot

Software - Programs the robot in Java

HPR - Fundraises and handles outreach, imagery, awards, admin, and recruitment

Strategy - Designs the the robot and plans match strategies

Safety - Safety trains all team members and supervises the shop
