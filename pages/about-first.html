What is FIRST

FIRST was founded in 1989 as a way for students of all ages to learn and enjoy STEM. Since 1989 over 4 million students and 110 countries have participated in FIRST programs. FIRST offers hands-on learning from the beginning of elementary school to the end of high school, allowing students to develop teamwork, confidence, and life skills throughout a student's lifetime. FIRST offers three main programs, FIRST Lego League (FLL) grades k-8, FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) grades 7-12, and FIRST Robotics Challenge (FRC) grades 9-12.

FIRST Robotics competition (FRC)

FRC is a competitive robotics competition where thousands of teams compete every year from across the globe. At the start of every year teams get a new game to design a robot for, teams then get 6 weeks to build, wire, and code these robots before competing with them. During this process teams have to learn how to persevere, work on a team, and creatively solve problems in a timely manner. Teams first compete at a regional or their local district event and the best teams from each competition go to the annual FIRST Championship, where 600 of the worlds best teams battle it out over 3 grueling days until a winning alliance of three teams is crowned victorious.

Gracious Professionalism®

is a part of the ethos of FIRST. It's a way of doing things that encourages high-quality work, emphasizes the value of others, and respects individuals and the community. Through Gracious Professionalism, fierce competition and mutual gain coexist. Participants compete intensely while treating each other with respect and empathy. There is no trash talking, nor disingenuous platitudes. Knowledge, competition, and empathy blend

Coopertition®

fosters innovation by promoting unqualified kindness and respect in the face of intense competition. At FIRST, Coopertition means teams help and cooperate with each other, even as they compete. It's about learning from teammates, teaching others, collaborating with mentors, managing, and being managed. Coopertition embodies the spirit of competing while assisting and enabling others whenever possible.
