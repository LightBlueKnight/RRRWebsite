Who We Are

FRC TEAMS 3006 AND 2726

Red Rock Robotics fields two FIRST Robotics Competition teams, Team 3006 and Team 2726, from Salt Lake City, Utah. We are a student-led organization that makes sure all students can participate without needing to pay any money. Our teams design and build robots from scratch each season, being a strong competitor amongst the thousands of FRC teams.
